# STRID

A dedicated AI operations team for high-tier IRL streamers.

## The problem

The biggest IRL operations in the world are run on memory and group chats. Locations chosen on the drive. Briefs that live in someone's head. Clips that go viral for reasons nobody captures. Knowledge that walks out the door when staff do.

The teams behind these streamers are good, but they're spread thin. The result is a ceiling on the operation that has nothing to do with the streamer's talent.

## What STRID does

STRID is the operation underneath the operation. A multi-agent system built around the work a top-tier streaming business actually needs done.

**Plans the stream.** Locations, hooks, segments, route, timing, risk flags.

**Listens to the audience.** Continuously, across chat, clips, donations, comments, and platforms. Not analytics — synthesis.

**Watches what goes viral, and why.** Clip mechanics studied across thousands of streams, fed back into every plan.

**Manages logistics.** Routes, hotels, gear, signal, borders. Handled in the background, surfaced only when a decision is needed.

**Knows the field.** Other streamers, collab dynamics, location archetypes, audience overlap. The context a great producer carries in their head — always on, never lost.

**Learns from every stream.** What worked, what didn't, why. The system gets sharper every time it runs.

## What changes

The plan exists before the stream does. The audience is being read continuously. Logistics are handled, not improvised. Viral clips stop feeling like accidents. Nothing important lives in any one person's head.

The streamer's job becomes the stream itself. Everything underneath it runs.

## Who it's for

Streamers running multi-platform IRL operations as a business. People whose current alternative is a full human team — and who want coverage that human teams can't match, without the cost, overhead, or knowledge loss when staff change.

Built top-down. Maximalist version first, lighter tiers later.


## The name

The Strid is a renowned stream in England that looks calm on the surface — but it's ferociously powerful underneath.

Most rivers do their work in plain sight. The Strid does its work where you can't see it.

Built for streamers who treat streaming like the business it is.
